# Shovey Island changelog

## [0.4.3] - 2026-10-20

- Use Classic's original round green player in every direction and preview.
- Keep Inner Breeze's bottom navigation visible while playing.

## [0.4.2] - 2026-10-20

- Every screen keeps Shovey's own slate, stone and green look, whatever app it runs in.

## [0.4.1] - 2026-10-20

- Swipe anywhere on the game screen to move, not only on the board.
- The top bar shows Levels, Restart and Help as icons, so the level's name fits.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-19

- Shovey Island now appears beside Shovey Classic and Shovey Editor as a Coming Soon edition.
- Island keeps the same Shovey as Classic and uses seamless whole tiles while it is being finished.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-19

- Soft sound effects for pushes, splashes, goals, pickups, openings and coming home on desktop.
- No move or piece counters under the board; the island's name shows instead.
- Restart always restarts the island you see, also in games saved before the new islands.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-18

- New artwork for every island tile and object, for Shovey and for the game card.
- 150 islands with new layouts and names; more come in later updates.
- Opening the game goes straight to your newest unlocked island; Islands
  still lets you replay any unlocked one.
- The board fills the screen in portrait and landscape, under one compact bar.
- Play by touch: swipe or tap on the board, or hold the on-screen joystick,
  which steps out of the way while you use a keyboard.
- Follows the light or dark look of the app it runs in.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-18

- Play Island in its own campaign of 175 islands.
- Continue your saved position, earn new levels and replay unlocked puzzles.
- Choose unlocked levels using whole-board previews; future levels stay locked.
